The largest #bear in the world and the Arctic's top predator,[^1] polar bears are a powerful symbol of **strength** and **endurance** of the [Arctic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic).

They spend most of their lives on the ==sea ice==, hunting seals from breathing holes. See also [[Arctic Expedition]].
